How we coach

A volleyball club should leave players better people than it found them. Everything we teach starts there.

Character comes first
Players learn to compete hard, lose well, lift up teammates and show up for each other. Those habits matter long after the final whistle.
Fun is part of the plan
Players improve fastest when they enjoy the work. Practices are energetic, and mistakes are how we learn.
The whole player
Passing, setting, hitting, serving, blocking and defense. We build every skill so players are not boxed into one position too early.
A love of the game
We want players who still choose volleyball because they love it, whether they play through high school or beyond.
Ready for the next level
For players who want to play in college, we help with the path: skills, film, exposure and honest guidance.

    Common questions

    My player has never played volleyball. Can they join?

    Yes. Our tryouts and open gyms are built for new players. We look for effort, attitude and a willingness to learn.

    How much does it cost?

    Fees depend on the program. Use the contact form or email us and we'll send current pricing. Ask us about options if cost is a concern.

    Where do you practice?

    We practice in Boone and the surrounding High Country. Exact gyms and times are shared with registered families.

    Do you help players who want to play in college?

    Yes. We help players build skills, understand the recruiting process and make smart choices. Take a look at the Next level section to see where our players have gone.

    How do I pay?

    Use the buttons in the Pay online section. You can pay for tryouts, dues, camps and tournaments by card.
